About This Toolkit

This toolkit provides exact calculations for quantum angular momentum coupling coefficients and rotation matrices. The Clebsch-Gordan coefficients are fundamental in quantum mechanics, appearing in the addition of angular momenta, selection rules for transitions, and the transformation between coupled and uncoupled basis states. The Wigner d-matrix describes rotations of angular momentum eigenstates.

All calculations use exact rational arithmetic (BigInt) to avoid floating-point precision issues. Results are displayed both as decimal values and in symbolic form (√p/q) when possible.

Features:
  • Half-integer quantum numbers (½, ³⁄₂, etc.)
  • Selection rule validation
  • Exact symbolic results
  • Full matrix display for Wigner d-function
  • Works offline — no server required
