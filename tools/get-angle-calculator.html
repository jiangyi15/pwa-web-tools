Helicity Amplitude Formula

For decay A → B + C, the angular part of the helicity amplitude is: Tl,s = √((2l+1)/(2JA+1)) × CG(JB,λB; JC,-λC | s,δ) × CG(l,0; s,δ | JA,δ) × DJA*λA,δ(φ,θ) where: • δ = λB - λC • l = orbital angular momentum (integer) • s = total spin of B+C system • D* = Wigner D-matrix conjugate Selection rules: • |JB-JC| ≤ s ≤ JB+JC • |l-s| ≤ JA ≤ l+s • |δ| ≤ JA
