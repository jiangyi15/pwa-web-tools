Angular Distribution I(θ,φ)

The angular distribution is: I(θ,φ) = Σλ,l,s |Tl,s|² where T is the helicity amplitude from the Angular Formula Calculator, and |T|² = (Re)² + (Im)². Per-LS decomposition: I(l,s)(θ,φ) = Σm Am(θ) · cos(mφ) + Bm(θ) · sin(mφ) with Am(θ) expressed in sinp(θ/2)·cosq(θ/2) form. Fourier expansion in φ: Trigonometric product identities expand cos(aφ)·cos(bφ) etc. into single cos/sin Fourier modes.
