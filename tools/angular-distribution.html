Angular Distribution I(θ,φ)

The angular distribution is: I(θ,φ) = Σλ,l,s |Tl,s|² where T is the helicity amplitude from the Angular Formula Calculator, and |T|² = (Re)² + (Im)². Per-LS decomposition: I(l,s)(θ,φ) = Σk Ak · cos(k·θ/2) · cos(k·φ/2) + Bk · sin(k·θ/2) · sin(k·φ/2) where sinp(θ/2)·cosq(θ/2) factors from the Wigner-d expansion are converted to cos(k·θ/2), sin(k·θ/2) Fourier basis via half-angle identities. All angles use the same half-angle basis cos(k·angle/2), sin(k·angle/2) with integer k. Fourier expansion in θ and φ: Trigonometric product identities expand cos(k₁θ/2)·cos(k₂θ/2) and cos(k₁φ/2)·cos(k₂φ/2) etc. into single cos/sin Fourier modes. The final result has at most one cos/sin factor per angle variable per term.
