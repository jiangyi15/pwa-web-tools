Conversion Formula

For decay A → B + C, helicity amplitudes expressed via LS coupling: HλB,λC = Σl,s gl,s · Cl,sλB,λC Cl,sλB,λC = √((2l+1)/(2JA+1)) · ⟨JBλB; JC,-λC | s,δ⟩ · ⟨l,0; s,δ | JA,δ⟩ where δ = λB − λC and |δ| ≤ JA. Selection rules:
  • |JB−JC| ≤ s ≤ JB+JC
  • |l−s| ≤ JA ≤ l+s
  • Parity: PA · PB · PC = (−1)l (if all three parities are set)
